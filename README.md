# 📷 ScanGenius - QR & Barcode Scanner

**ScanGenius** is a fast, 100% offline Android app that lets you scan QR codes, generate custom QR codes (Text, URL, Wi-Fi, Contact), and manage your scan history. Perfect for everyday scanning without annoying pop-ups or slowdowns.

## ✨ Features
- 🔍 **Fast Scanning:** Instantly scan QR codes and barcodes using your camera.
- 🖼️ **Scan from Gallery:** Import a screenshot or photo and extract the QR code.
- 🔦 **Flashlight Toggle:** Scan easily in dark environments.
- ✍️ **Custom QR Generator:** Create QR codes for Text, URLs, Wi-Fi networks, and Contact cards.
- 🎨 **Custom Colors:** Choose the color of your generated QR codes (Black, Blue, Red, Green).
- 📜 **History:** Automatically save scanned and generated codes in a local database.
- 🌐 **Auto-Open Links:** Optionally open scanned URLs directly in your browser.
- 🌙 **Dark Mode:** Full Material 3 support with an elegant dark theme.
- 🔒 **100% Offline & Private:** All scans and history stay on your device.

## 🚀 Installation
You can install ScanGenius directly on your Android device.

1. Go to the **[Releases](https://github.com/CartonIQ/ScanGenius/releases)** page.
2. Download the latest `ScanGenius.apk` file.
3. Open the file on your phone and tap **Install** (you may need to allow "Install from unknown sources").

## 🛠️ Built With
- **Language:** Kotlin
- **UI:** Material 3, XML Layouts, ViewBinding
- **Architecture:** MVVM, Navigation Component
- **Local Database:** Room DB
- **Scanning Engine:** ZXing (JourneyApps)
- **QR Generation:** ZXing Core
- **Monetization:** Google AdMob (Banner, Interstitial, Native)
